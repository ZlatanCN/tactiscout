# Keep performance evidence source-specific

Additional player metrics remain observations owned by their provider, keyed by that provider's player, team, season, and position group. Evidence can also be a deterministic aggregate of source events, but must preserve the source fields, formula, sample and coverage rules. TactiScout may cite these values as evidence, but they do not enter a role score until an explicit, documented formula exists; matching names alone never merges records across providers.
